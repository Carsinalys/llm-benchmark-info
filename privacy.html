Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · App: LLMRally (macOS)

Summary

LLMRally collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app does not track you, and nothing about your benchmark results, model usage, or configuration leaves your device.

LLMRally connects only to the inference endpoints you configure yourself — typically a local server running on http://127.0.0.1 on your own Mac. The app has no built-in remote servers and no telemetry endpoints.

Data the app stores — and why

Backend configuration The list of inference server addresses you've added (Ollama, LM Studio, etc.) so the app can auto-detect and connect to them. Stored in Apple's local user-defaults.
Benchmark results Speed metrics (tokens/sec, TTFT), quality scores per test category, and the model's responses to test items. Saved as JSON files in your Mac's Application Support/LLMRally/ folder so you can view run history and radar charts. Never uploaded.
Notes and tags Free-text notes and tag words you add to a benchmark result (e.g. "Q4 quant, 8k context") to help you organize your history. Stored locally with the result.
App settings Preferred backend and model selection. Stored in Apple's local user-defaults so the app picks up where you left off.

What the app connects to

Local inference servers Yes — only the endpoints you configure. Typically http://127.0.0.1:11434 (Ollama), http://127.0.0.1:1234 (LM Studio), etc. Used to send benchmark prompts and receive responses. No data is transmitted to these servers other than the test prompts and the model's actual output.
App Store (in-app purchases) Yes — only when you choose to buy Deep Testing or restore a purchase. Handled by Apple's StoreKit framework. The developer never sees your payment details.
Analytics / tracking Never — no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no fingerprinting.
Remote servers Never — the app contains no built-in remote endpoints of any kind.

Third-party software

LLMRally contains zero third-party dependencies. The app is built entirely with Apple's frameworks (SwiftUI, StoreKit, Foundation). There are no SDKs from any analytics provider, ad network, or tracking service — so none of them receive any data.

What the app does NOT do

  • Does not require an account or sign-in
  • Does not upload your benchmark results
  • Does not upload your model's responses
  • Does not download or install models
  • Does not run models itself
  • Does not read files outside its own sandbox container
  • Does not transmit any data beyond the endpoints you configure

Permissions the app requests

App Sandbox Required by macOS for App Store distribution. LLMRally's data stays inside its sandbox container.
Outgoing network Used to connect to your local inference endpoints (HTTP requests to 127.0.0.1 or other addresses you configure). No incoming connections are accepted.
User-selected file access Used only when you choose to export a benchmark result (CSV, JSON, or PNG). The app writes to a file location you explicitly pick in a save dialog. It can never access files you haven't directed it to.

Deleting your data

Your benchmark results live as JSON files in ~/Library/Containers/com.llmrally.app/Data/Library/Application Support/LLMRally/records/. You can delete them from inside the app (select a model in the results list, then click "Delete results") or by removing that folder manually. The app stores nothing anywhere else.

Children's privacy

LLMRally is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from any user of any age, because it does not collect any information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected in the App Store description.
